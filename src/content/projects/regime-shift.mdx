## Problem

Market regimes can alter the relationship between risk and allocation. This project combines Gaussian HMM inference with regime statistics and portfolio weights.

## Architecture

Feature preparation, HMM fitting, regime estimation, allocation, rebalancing, and reporting are separate modules. A rolling or expanding walk-forward validator refits the scaler and HMM for each training fold.

<Diagram steps="Training window|Regime inference|Portfolio simulation" />

## Implementation

Training-only scaler fitting is implemented. However, the entire test window is passed to Viterbi decoding and forward-backward posterior inference. The portfolio simulator then applies date-specific weights to that same date's returns.

## Limitations

Future observations within a test sequence can affect smoothed state estimates. Same-day signal/return timing also needs correction or explicit justification. Failed folds are skipped after logging, so the successful-fold denominator matters. Fixed-universe and capacity assumptions remain research limitations.
