## Problem

Research signals need an accounting and execution context before their behavior can be interpreted. AQC separates these responsibilities across an event-driven framework.

## Architecture

The BacktestEngine coordinates market, signal, order, and fill events. A separate PaperBroker registers orders, checks available market context, simulates partial fills, and updates order state.

<Diagram steps="Market events|Order lifecycle|Simulated fills" />

## Implementation

The backtester preloads market columns into NumPy arrays. PaperBroker delegates fills to a simulator and charges commission; the inspected call supplies a fixed daily-volatility assumption and a fallback volume when reported volume is absent.

## Results

VERIFIED by source inspection: event orchestration and paper-broker state transitions exist. Directory names such as institutional, deployment, and live_capital do not prove institutional use or real-money trading.

## Limitations

Bar-by-bar iteration alone does not guarantee causality when a strategy can access data arrays. Same-bar fill timing, missing-volume defaults, fixed volatility, multiple-testing controls, and calibration require scrutiny before performance interpretation.
