## Problem

The project explores ranking résumé text against a job-description query.

## Implementation

The inspected matcher creates a 384-dimensional FAISS IndexFlatIP, normalizes document and query vectors, and returns nearest-neighbor scores and identifiers.

## Results

VERIFIED by source inspection: normalized inner-product retrieval is implemented. Ranking quality and hiring validity have not been established.

## Limitations

Similarity is not evidence of job performance or candidate suitability. Document privacy, retrieval evaluation, and input handling remain deployment considerations.
