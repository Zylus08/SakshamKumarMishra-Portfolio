## Problem

A surrogate can approximate simulator outputs, but its usefulness depends on the simulator's physical assumptions and evaluation protocol.

## Architecture

NumPy-based kinematics and event simulation feed a dataset pipeline. A PyTorch surrogate trains on saved arrays, and a dashboard presents simulations and results.

<Diagram steps="Sample events|Kinematic simulator|Learned surrogate" />

## Implementation

The physics module computes momentum magnitudes and invariant mass and provides a simplified two-body energy calculation. The training script uses MSE, Adam, shuffled minibatches, and a held-out array for evaluation.

## Results

VERIFIED: the kinematic functions and surrogate training loop are implemented. No independently reproduced speedup, detector realism, or physical-discovery result is claimed.

## Limitations

The module explicitly leaves full frame transformations and detector noise as future work. Clamping final-state energies is not a general conservation proof for forbidden configurations. Training reproducibility and out-of-distribution validation require further work.
