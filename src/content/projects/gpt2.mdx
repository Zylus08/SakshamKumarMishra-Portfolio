## Problem

Implementing the attention mechanism directly makes its masking and tensor transformations easier to study.

## Implementation

CausalSelfAttention projects queries, keys, and values, reshapes them into heads, applies a lower-triangular mask, uses scaled dot-product attention and dropout, then projects the merged heads.

## Results

VERIFIED: the attention mechanism exists in source. The repository also contains assistant/provider abstractions, but no pretrained-GPT-2 equivalence or independently trained language-model quality is claimed.

## Limitations

Architectural similarity does not establish weight compatibility, benchmark parity, or a new model contribution.
