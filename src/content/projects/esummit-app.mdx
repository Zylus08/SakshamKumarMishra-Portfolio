## Problem

An event app brings schedules, ticket access, and participant-facing information into one mobile interface.

## Implementation

TicketService queries Firestore, streams tickets by authenticated user, parses a structured QR payload, compares ownership, and marks a ticket as used.

## Results

VERIFIED: ticket and QR handling exist, with commits attributed to Zylus08 for ticketing and accessibility work. Attendance, production adoption, and security of deployed rules are unverified.

## Limitations

The inspected check-then-update ticket redemption is not a transaction, so concurrent scans need review. Client logic does not substitute for server-side authorization and Firestore rules.
