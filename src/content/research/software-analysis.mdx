## Research question

How far can static semantic evidence support change proposals while keeping uncertainty and execution boundaries explicit?

## Methodology

The collaborative Thermodynamic Evolution system combines a Rust Tree-sitter structural analyzer with Go semantic summaries, proposal orchestration, and sandbox-mediated evaluation. Structural energy is a code metric; it is not physical energy or a demonstrated runtime speedup.

## Results

Source inspection confirms summary-cache and execution-gate implementations. The latest verification report concerns a bounded fixture corpus and explicitly defers autonomous remediation. Its reported fixture results were not rerun here.

## Limitations

Fixture success does not prove soundness on arbitrary programs. Unresolved ownership and interface dispatch remain conservative cases. No accepted paper or general security guarantee is claimed.
