## Research question

Can temporal structure help learn useful EEG representations without relying on labels for every training example?

## Methodology

The public SleepApnea extension implements temporal contrastive objectives and an ablation pipeline with subject-level train, validation, and test partitions. Self-supervised fitting uses the training partition; validation selects checkpoints before test evaluation. This is an extension of a forked dataset repository, not ownership of the original dataset.

## Results

The repository’s final experimental-status report invalidates earlier multiseed rankings after a seed-handling bug. Its corrected runs show substantial variability and do not support a robust improvement claim. These are documented findings, not training runs reproduced in this portfolio audit.

## Limitations

No clinical effectiveness, diagnostic accuracy, transfer benefit, publication acceptance, or independent replication is established. Subject-level partitions alone do not settle all preprocessing and repeated-selection risks.
