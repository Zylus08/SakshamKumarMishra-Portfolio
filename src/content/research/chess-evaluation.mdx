## Research question

When does blending a learned evaluator with a classical chess evaluator help under a fixed search budget?

## Methodology

Project Janus contains a native search implementation and an optional NNUE evaluator with accumulator code. The inspected phase blend scales neural influence with material phase; classical evaluation remains the documented default.

## Results

The v0.65 repository report records exploratory comparisons. Its intervals and testing conditions do not establish an official rating or a reliably positive strength gain. The portfolio audit inspected source and the report but did not repeat tournaments.

## Limitations

Opening selection, time controls, opponents, seeds, confidence intervals, and independent held-out matches are needed before promoting strength claims. A manuscript described as ready is not a publication.
