## Count definitions and limits

- RiskLab: 13 tests passed in the 9 November 2026 offline subset, recorded in the [M3 provenance audit](M3-CLAIM-PROVENANCE.md#risklab) and retained `.tmp/m3-risklab-tests.log` (13 passed in 0.88s). Four selected modules cover accounting, prefix stability, volatility and metrics. This is a historical test result, not a fresh rerun, entire-suite count or investment result.
- Kamish: five broker operation types: ingress, replication, export, fetch and promotion. Source-reviewed enum/policy scope, not throughput or distributed deployment validation. Collaborative work; the card identifies the personal integration contribution.
- Thermodynamic: four code metrics: cyclomatic complexity, maximum depth, AST node count and concurrency primitive count. Implemented analysis outputs, not measured energy savings.
- EEG: three temporal weighting kernels: exponential, clipped linear and cutoff. Implemented alternatives, not three successful clinical experiments.

Other cards use structural evidence instead of forced numbers. No P99, profit, Sharpe, Elo, clinical accuracy, manuscript-only benchmark or illustrative workload number was promoted. Source links are checked against both article bodies and the independently recorded pinned-tree fixture; this validates provenance paths, not scientific correctness.
