# M4.1 visual QA

Date: 2026-11-11. Local production build only.

## Design decisions

The hero preserves its serif emphasis, authentic portrait and asterisk. Concrete positioning replaces the previous general introduction, and a small Quant résumé link replaces the secondary About CTA. The main selected-work CTA remains.

Career pathways sit inside the existing engineering act. A restrained heading, four outlined selectors and three editorial columns use the existing warm accent, surfaces, text and line colors. At 900 pixels and below, selectors wrap into two columns and entries form a single reading column. At 320 pixels the decorative selector numbers are omitted to preserve label space. The entire role label remains visible. No gradients, counters, new imagery, popups or additional animation library were introduced.

## Verification record

The full 116-test browser suite passed. All four selections at 320, 390, 768 and 1440 pixels in both themes passed horizontal-overflow checks and WCAG A/AA axe scans with zero violations. Keyboard selection, focus order and reduced-motion checks passed. The six focused tests passed again after correcting the capture harness.

Sixteen images record the hero and active Quant panel at each size/theme. Representative hero/panel captures at all four widths were visually inspected, spanning both themes. The hero copy and direct résumé CTA are legible, portraits retain their framing, selectors stay visible, and reading lists wrap into a single column on narrow screens. At 320 pixels the Quant label wraps cleanly to two lines. Document and archive links remain separate and readable. No app styling fix was needed after inspection.

The initial tall element captures were clipped or occluded by the sticky header. Final component captures use an 1800px-high viewport and a 120px top offset; hero captures and responsive assertions use 900px height. This is a capture correction, not a hidden page element or application layout change. No header is removed from the page.
