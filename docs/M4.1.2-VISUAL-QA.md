# M4.1.2 — Visual QA

Captured from local production builds before and after this milestone, using Chrome, reduced motion and loaded web fonts. Hero viewports are 1440×900 and 390×900; pathway and Engineering images are element captures at the same widths. Engineering comparison shows the first featured card (TradeCore), not the entire seven-card section. Screenshot pairs retain original dimensions; compare at equal zoom.

## Findings

- Hero: the original H1, photograph and actions remain. Four compact experience entries precede the generative field, in four columns on desktop and two on mobile. At 390×900 all four entries are visible near the bottom of the first viewport. Longer internship titles wrap without overlap. The strip links to Journey instead of duplicating its narrative.
- Pathways: shorter introductions and descriptors reduce reading time and panel height. Four tabs, three article links and contextual PDF access remain. The default Quant panel is shown; automated tests exercise every panel.
- Engineering: the original visual and typography remain. Implementation-first copy is followed by a separately styled evidence label. The card grows modestly to accommodate explicit provenance without removing article access. Quant ordering is verified by DOM assertions.
- Both themes preserve contrast and framing. No clipping or horizontal overflow was seen in the reviewed captures. The experience strip remains understated; it does not introduce badges or counters.
- The 1200×630 social image retains the poetic identity, adds the full name and uses the Quant/ML/research positioning. All text fits within the canvas.

## Coverage limits

The new automated scan covers hero, Engineering and Research with WCAG A/AA rules at 320, 390, 768 and 1440px in both themes, reduced motion, horizontal overflow and stable strip height. Existing suite coverage includes pathways, Journey, navigation and other pages. Automated axe checks and screenshot inspection do not constitute manual screen-reader testing or measured field Core Web Vitals. No claim of production deployment parity is made.
